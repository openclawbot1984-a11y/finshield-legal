## 2. What FinShield Does

FinShield is an Android security app that:

- Scans apps installed on your device for permission patterns associated with financial fraud
- Monitors which apps are active in the foreground while you use banking or UPI apps
- Alerts you when potentially risky behaviour is detected

FinShield performs all scanning on your device. It does not have access to your banking credentials, UPI PINs, or transaction history.

---

## 3. What FinShield Does Not Do

FinShield is **not**:

- A guaranteed protection against all financial fraud
- A replacement for antivirus software or Google Play Protect
- A real-time transaction monitor or banking app
- A service that can block or stop a fraudulent transaction in progress

FinShield warns you about risky app patterns. What you do with those warnings is your decision.

## 4. No Warranty on Detection Accuracy

FinShield's detection is based on static app analysis (declared permissions, install source) and foreground app monitoring. It does not inspect what apps actually do at runtime, read your SMS messages, or access your banking data.

**FinShield may miss threats.** A malicious app may not trigger any of FinShield's signals. FinShield may also flag apps that are not actually harmful (false positives).
